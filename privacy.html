Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Mindless Mini Games iPhone app. It's written to be read, not skimmed past.

Effective September 4, 2026

The short version

No account, no sign-inThe app has no login, no profile, and no way to identify you.
No name, email, or contactsNever asked for, never collected.
No location, camera, microphone, or photosThe app requests none of these permissions. iOS would have to prompt you, and it never will.
No tracking across apps or websitesThe app does not request Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), so you won't see the "Allow Tracking" prompt.
Game data stays on your deviceScores, canvases, unlocked themes and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone.
Ads are served by Google AdMobNon-personalized. AdMob receives limited technical data to deliver and measure the ad — details below.
Purchases go through AppleApple processes the payment. RevenueCat records the receipt so the app knows you own Remove Ads.

What the app stores on your phone

Everything you build up while playing is written to your device's local storage and stays there:

  • Best scores and records for each game and mode
  • Canvas points, which canvas you're filling in, and which canvases you've completed
  • Sparks, unlocked themes and costumes, and daily-challenge progress
  • Sound and music toggles and other in-app settings
  • A small local tally of how many ads were shown in each game, used only to draw the app's own insights screen

None of this is sent to a server, because there isn't one. It has no name attached to it and nothing that identifies you. Deleting the app deletes all of it — permanently, with no backup on my end. There is no cloud sync and no Game Center integration.

Advertising

The app shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. To deliver, cap, and measure those ads, Google's SDK receives technical information from the device such as your approximate (coarse, IP-derived) location, device model and OS version, and ad interaction events like impressions and clicks.

Non-personalized only

The app is configured to request non-personalized ads, and it does not request Apple's advertising identifier. Ads are chosen from general context rather than a profile of you. Google may still use limited data for frequency capping, aggregate reporting, and fraud prevention, as described in their policies.

Your consent choices

Where the law requires it — the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, and certain US states — the app presents Google's consent form (the UMP / Funding Choices dialog) the first time you play, and your choice is remembered. You can bring that form back at any time from the app's settings. Google's handling of ad data is covered by the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob policies.

Apple's SKAdNetwork

The app includes SKAdNetwork identifiers, an Apple framework that reports ad campaign performance in aggregate. It's designed by Apple specifically so advertisers can measure installs without identifying individual users, and it runs at the operating-system level.

Purchases

Mindless Mini Games offers one optional purchase: Remove Ads, a one-time, non-consumable unlock. Apple's App Store processes the payment; I never see your card details, billing address, or Apple ID.

RevenueCat is used to validate and remember that purchase so it restores correctly on your devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and Apple's transaction receipt. It does not receive your name or email. Their handling is covered by the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Analytics

There is no third-party analytics SDK in this app — no Firebase Analytics, no crash-reporting service, no attribution SDK. The only "analytics" is a counter that lives entirely on your phone, tallying ad impressions and estimated revenue per game so the app can draw its own insights screen. It contains no identifiers and no timestamps, and it is never transmitted anywhere.

Children

Mindless Mini Games is not directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone and serves only non-personalized ads, there is nothing to delete on request — but if you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please write to me and I'll look into it.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, or delete personal information a company holds about you. I hold none: there is no server, no database, and no record of you. The practical version of those rights here is this — deleting the app erases everything the app ever saved. For data Google or Apple hold in connection with ads or purchases, use their own privacy tools, linked above.

I do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are used in US state privacy laws.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted on this page with a new effective date. Meaningful changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
