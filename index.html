M Mindless Mini Games

The legal bit, in plain English.

Mindless Mini Games is a small collection of quiet little games. It doesn't want your email address, your contacts, or your location — and it doesn't ask for any of them.

Last updated September 4, 2026

The one-paragraph version

There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your scores, your canvases and your settings live on your iPhone and nowhere else. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and those ads are non-personalized — Apple's advertising identifier is never requested. If you buy Remove Ads, Apple handles the payment and RevenueCat tells the app you own it. That's the whole story; the pages below just say it more carefully.
